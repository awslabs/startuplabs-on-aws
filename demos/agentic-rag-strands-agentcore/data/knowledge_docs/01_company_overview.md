# NovaTech Solutions - Company Overview

## About Us

NovaTech Solutions is a cloud-native software company founded in 2019, headquartered in Seattle, Washington. We specialize in building enterprise-grade AI and machine learning platforms for mid-to-large organizations.

## Mission

Our mission is to democratize AI adoption by providing intuitive, scalable, and secure platforms that empower teams to build, deploy, and monitor ML models without requiring deep infrastructure expertise.

## Core Products

### NovaPlatform
Our flagship product — an end-to-end ML platform that handles data ingestion, feature engineering, model training, deployment, and monitoring. Supports both batch and real-time inference workloads.

### NovaInsight
A business intelligence layer powered by AI that provides natural language querying over enterprise data warehouses. Supports SQL, NoSQL, and graph databases.

### NovaGuard
An AI safety and governance toolkit that provides model evaluation, bias detection, fairness auditing, and explainability reports for production ML models.
